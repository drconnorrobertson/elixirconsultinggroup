Welcome to Elixir Consulting Group

At Elixir Consulting Group, we specialize in helping entrepreneurs and business buyers navigate the complex world of business acquisitions. Whether you're looking to acquire your first business or build a multi-company portfolio, we provide expert guidance on every aspect of the acquisition process.

Founded by Connor Robertson, an experienced acquisitions consultant and author, we combine practical deal-making experience with proven frameworks for success. Our approach emphasizes deep due diligence, strategic integration, and long-term value creation.

What We Cover

Deal Identification

Finding the right acquisition targets that match your investment criteria and financial goals.

Due Diligence

Comprehensive analysis including financial, operational, legal, and market assessment of acquisition targets.

Valuation

Data-driven valuation methods that go beyond simple multiples to determine fair acquisition prices.

Negotiation

Strategic negotiation tactics that protect your interests while building productive seller relationships.

Integration

Post-acquisition integration planning and execution to maximize value and minimize disruption.

Portfolio Building

Strategies for acquiring multiple businesses and consolidating them into larger, more valuable enterprises.
